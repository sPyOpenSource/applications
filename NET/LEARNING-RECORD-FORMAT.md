# Learning Record Format

Title: `NNNN-dash-case-name.md`

## Context
What led to this learning/insight?

## Discovery
What did I learn? Key facts/concepts.

## Insight
Non-obvious connections, patterns, or realizations.

## Zone of Proximal Development Implication
How does this change what I should learn next?

## Actionable Takeaway
Concrete thing I can do with this knowledge.
